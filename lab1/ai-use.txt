4.One of the issues I ran into was making the request-form.html page to look uniform when on a dekstop and when on a mobile 
version of the page. I had seen in the lecture how to change the columns for different form objects but didn't understand it 
fully. I asked the AI assitant to explain the details of the class= "col-md-6" syntax. It clarified that the md indicates that
the command only applies to large screens. Each section is split into 12 untis, so the -6 indicates for the column (col) to 
take up half of the total space when in a larger width. This is applied to both the date label and the course label meaning 
in a large view (like a desktop) the each column takes up half of the section. Then when seen in a smaller width view, the 
rule does not apply and it stacks the sections making it more easily viewed. After applying that syntax, I verified in the 
test web page and it functioned just as described.
